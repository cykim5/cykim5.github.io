Projects

Gene-ius

Nucleate Pittsburgh BioHack 2026, team of five

An AI agent that helps laboratories interpret genetic variants of uncertain significance, which account for roughly a third of variants in ClinVar. Across 23 retina and heart genes it ranks which variants to test first and explains whether a variant is harmful, how it breaks the protein, which disease follows, and what treatment strategy might fit.

Trained only on data available in 2023 and graded on variants experts resolved later, it identified 38 proven disease variants in its top 500, against 18 for AlphaMissense.

OT-2 Tilting Plate Adapter

Laboratory hardware, CAD and 3D printing

A 3D printed adapter that occupies one deck slot of an Opentrons OT-2 liquid handling robot and tilts the plate so liquid pools to one side of each well. Two stepper motors turn cams beneath a platform pivoting on a fixed ball, giving tilt in any direction up to 17 degrees.

Built as a parametric CAD model and verified in simulation across 25 motor positions for collisions before printing.
