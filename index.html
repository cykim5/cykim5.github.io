Projects

Gene-ius

Nucleate Pittsburgh BioHack 2026, team of 5

An AI agent that helps labs interpret genetic variants of uncertain significance (VUS), which make up about a third of variants in ClinVar. For variants in 23 retina and heart genes, it ranks which ones to test first and explains whether a variant is harmful, how it breaks the protein, which disease follows, and what treatment strategy might fit.

Tested by training only on 2023 data and grading on variants experts resolved later. It found 38 later-proven disease variants in its top 500, compared with 18 for AlphaMissense.

OT-2 Tilting Plate Adapter

Lab hardware, CAD and 3D printing

A 3D printed adapter that sits in one deck slot of an Opentrons OT-2 liquid handling robot and tilts the plate, so liquid pools to one side of each well. Two stepper motors turn cams under the platform, which pivots on a fixed ball, so it can tilt in any direction, up to 17 degrees.

Built as a parametric CAD model and checked in simulation across 25 motor positions for collisions before printing.
